# Changelog

Human-readable log of what changed in the onboarding prototype, for product review. Updated at each local commit — most recent first.

## 2026-09-25 — Rebranded for GIVA

This prototype now shows GIVA, the jewellery brand, end to end, on a fresh repository with no earlier brand's content, imagery or history in it.

**What GIVA runs on.** Checked on the live site: Shopify, with a Meta pixel and a Google Ads tag. The Shopify, Meta Ads and Google Merchant Center connectors already in the prototype are all correct, so no connector names changed.

**Brand.** GIVA's own wordmark, its rose and pink palette with sand, and Lato, the typeface giva.co uses. These carry through the workspace badge, Brand guidelines card, story rings and slides, the intro card glow (recoloured from green to rose), and the Upgrade, Credits and Jam windows.

**Feed and deck.** Every card is now a GIVA recommendation. The storefront, catalog and pricing cards come from things that are true on giva.co today:
- 5,853 of the 5,861 listings show a strike-through price, and the median markdown is 50%
- 321 of the 323 Rakhi listings are still live and in stock, about a month after Raksha Bandhan
- 578 listings have a size in the title, and 16 designs are split across 34 listings, one per size
- the Rose Gold Deer Heart Necklace, fourth in Bestsellers, gives one set of dimensions in its copy and a different set in its own measurement photo, and its gallery opens on a packshot, with the worn shot third
- the lifetime plating and 6-month warranty, 300+ stores and Anushka Sharma don't appear anywhere on the homepage; the BIS hallmark and the authenticity certificate appear only in the footer

**Brand Memory.** About, voice, company (founded 2019 by Ishendra Agarwal, Nikita Prasad and Aditya Labroo, Bengaluru, 300+ stores), catalog (5,861 live products, IXIA by GIVA), bestsellers and competitors (CaratLane, Tanishq, BlueStone, Palmonas).

**Imagery.** Creative cards and Popular-ads stories use GIVA's own campaign photography from the ShopOS shoot in the shared Drive: warm, editorial and worn on skin, or still life on satin. Catalog cards use GIVA's own product photos. The v2 and v3 asset sets are now real alternate frames of the same piece, no longer placeholder copies.

**Video.** Cards can now carry a looping, muted film. Two GIVA product films from the Drive are wired in: the leaf bracelet (Reels ad card) and the lotus jhumkis (storefront card).

**Known limits.**
- The AI-visibility numbers (score 38, share of voice, citation counts, sources) are placeholders shaped like the report format. No GEO audit has been run for GIVA.
- Ad spend, frequency and CPA figures on the performance cards, and the Signals customer counts, are placeholders too.
- The intro card's copy is unchanged. It is still to be decided separately.
- The Drive creatives aren't matched to specific GIVA product names, so those cards describe the piece ("gold teardrop pendant") rather than naming a SKU.
