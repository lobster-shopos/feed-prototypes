# Changelog

## v1 (1 Oct 2026): first Larkspire feed

What this is: the feed rebuilt for Larkspire, a made-up premium home developer,
so we can show the prototype to real-estate brands without naming one.

- **Brand.** Larkspire builds luxury towers, premium apartments, villas and plotted
  estates in twelve Indian cities. Four projects carry the feed: Solace (Gurugram,
  from ₹10.3 Cr), Tidewater (Mumbai, sea-facing), Crown Court (Hyderabad, from
  ₹1.29 Cr) and Greenhollow (Bengaluru, forest living).
- **Look.** Ink green, brass and limestone, Cormorant Garamond headings, a spire
  logo. The intro post, story rings and workspace badge use these colours.
- **Setup screen.** The Brand Memory column now reads Larkspire: about, brand kit,
  voice, company, catalog, projects, audience and four category leaders.
- **Feed and deck.** 16 cards across the four agents, each a recommendation tied to
  the festive calendar (Navratri from 11 Oct, Dussehra 20 Oct, Diwali 8 Nov), NRI
  demand, lead quality and AI search.
- **Signals.** Three real-estate signals: site visitors due a second visit,
  homeowners getting first pick of a new tower, and enquiries that went quiet.
- **Connectors.** The storefront connector is "Connect your CMS" everywhere; ads are
  Meta and Google only. The CRM sources are Sales CRM, Email and SMS, WhatsApp and
  Customer care.
- **One variant only,** so the v1/v2/v3 switch is gone.
- **Imagery.** Every feed, story and pop-up image is a free Unsplash photo, chosen per
  card: exteriors, interiors, amenities, people, plans and the city, instead of renders
  from two projects.

Known limits:

- All figures are invented but sized for a large listed developer.
- Two card captions still describe the old images ("golden-hour film", "terrace-couple
  cut") and will be fixed in the content pass.
- No video yet.
