# Changelog

Human-readable log of what changed in the Van Heusen Innerwear feed prototype, for product review. Updated at each local commit, most recent first.

## 2026-09-25 — Van Heusen Innerwear feed, first version

**What it is.** The ShopOS Feed prototype for Van Heusen Innerwear (vanheusenindia.abfrl.in): URL onboarding, live setup, Brand Memory, the feed, and the Pro deck with its Signals column. Every card, story and setup line is about this brand only, men-led, grounded in facts read off the live site.

**Known limits.** Figures on the ads, visibility and Signals cards are realistic placeholders, not measured Van Heusen data. See README.md for the brand facts, palette and asset sources this build uses.
