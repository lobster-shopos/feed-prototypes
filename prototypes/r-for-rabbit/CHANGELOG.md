# Changelog

Human-readable log of what changed in the R for Rabbit feed prototype, for product review. Most recent first.

## 2026-09-25 — First R for Rabbit build

The feed now runs as R for Rabbit end to end: onboarding, Brand Memory, the feed, the Pro deck, stories, history, and the Signals journeys.

- **Brand.** Real logo and rabbit mark, olive/red/white palette and Nunito, all read off rforrabbit.com. The workspace badge, side panel, story rings, intro-card wash and Upgrade/Jam modal images all carry R for Rabbit now.
- **Brand Memory.** Nine cards written from the brand's own site: safety-first positioning, voice, company (2014, 200+ people, 5M+ parents), 984-product catalog, the ISOFIX Plus and Feather Diapers, audience, and four category rivals.
- **Feed and deck.** Every card is a recommendation for R for Rabbit to approve. The catalog findings are real: no GTIN on any of 1,682 variants, 309 product titles over 90 characters, 888 variants with no shipping weight, and the i-Size R129 comparison sitting third in the ISOFIX Plus gallery.
- **Imagery.** Catalog cards use the brand's own product photos (ISOFIX Plus, Feather Diapers, tricycle). Creative cards use the brand's Drive campaign shots.
- **Video.** Two real clips from the brand's Drive are now posts: a diaper-change moment with the Feather Diapers pack in frame, and an apparel cut (fabric, worn, flat-lay). Cards can now carry video, not just images.
- **Signals.** The email source is CleverTap, which is what R for Rabbit actually runs; journeys pair a first order with Feather Diapers and give repeat buyers early access to an ISOFIX launch.
- **One variant only,** so the v1/v2/v3 switch is gone.

### Known limits

- AI visibility, ad-spend, frequency and Signals numbers are illustrative placeholders, not measured.
- The intro post sequence is unchanged, pending a separate decision.
- A third Drive clip (a studio apparel shot) was not used; two videos was enough for this pass.
- The diaper video's poster frame crops to the parent rather than the baby; it plays through to the full scene.
- No git yet. This folder has no history or remote until one is asked for.
